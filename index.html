Set aside for the redesign

Markup that fails identically on 3 or more sites, a theme component that renders an empty element, or a social icon link (an SVG inside a link) with no accessible name, counts as template and is left out above. Errors from PDFs opened in the browser's PDF viewer are also left out; they need fixing in the PDF files.
