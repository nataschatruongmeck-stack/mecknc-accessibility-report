Set aside for the redesign

Markup that fails identically on 3 or more sites, a theme component that renders an empty element, or a social icon link (an SVG inside a link) with no accessible name, counts as template and is left out above. Errors from PDFs opened in the browser's PDF viewer, and from documents hosted on mecknc.widen.net, are also left out. These are documents rather than webpages and need fixing in the files themselves.
